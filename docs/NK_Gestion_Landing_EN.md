## // 03 — TECHNICAL CAPABILITY
### Technical studies and analysis

- **Noise and vibration**  
  Environmental noise monitoring (D.S. 38 MMA), occupational noise (D.S. 594 MINSAL), fauna-component noise (SEA-MMA), underwater noise, illuminance and vibration monitoring.

- **Air quality**  
  PM10, PM2.5, PM1 and TSP (DS 61/2008), settleable particulate matter, process gas monitoring, on-site meteorology.

- **Water component**  
  Physicochemical water analysis and hydrogeological monitoring.

- **Geophysics and fieldwork**  
  VES, ERT, TEM, and SP surveys up to 300 m depth. Exploratory drilling, test pits, soil mechanics, RTK topography, drone surveys, and bathymetry.

- **Biotic environment**  
  Terrestrial and aquatic flora and fauna surveys with SAG and SUBPESCA-certified consultants, soil biology, and macroinvertebrates.

- **Social environment**  
  Social risk analysis, social studies, and public participation processes (PAC).

### Georeferenced, traceable data
Each measurement is recorded and documented on-site, with full traceability for technical and regulatory purposes.
